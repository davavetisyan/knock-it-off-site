Knock It Off!

Be the cat. Knock things off the shelf while she is not looking, and freeze when she turns around.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Send us a request (a free GitHub account is needed) and include your device model and iOS version. We reply within a few days.

FAQ

Is my progress saved? Stars, best scores, coins, cats and hats are saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. Ads need an internet connection, but the game itself works offline.

I bought something on another device. Open Cats & Hats, go to the Store tab and tap Restore.

How do I play? Tap the shelf to knock things off. When she turns around, stop tapping. Caught three times and you are carried out of the room.
